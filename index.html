What is Web Hosting?

Web hosting is the service that stores all your website files — like text, images, and videos — on a special computer called a server. This allows your website to be visible and accessible on the internet to anyone, anywhere.

How Web Hosting Works

You (Website Creator) → Upload files → Web Server → Connected to the Internet → Visitors Access Your Website

When you build a website, you upload your files to a web host. The host connects your files to the internet through servers. When users type your domain name, their browser fetches your website from the hosting server and displays it instantly.

Why Web Hosting Is Important

  • Makes websites accessible around the world 🌍
  • Stores your data safely on secure servers 🔒
  • Keeps websites fast and online 24/7 ⚡
  • Protects from cyberattacks and data loss 🧠
  • Provides backups and domain-based email 📧
